# DESIGN.md - Stark Bathroom Premium Minimalist (Haiman Faucet Style) Design Contract

## 1. Brand Concept & Aesthetic Target
- **Concept**: Nan'an Yingdu Stark Hardware Co., Ltd. - Global Premier OEM/ODM sanitary ware exporter.
- **Reference Vibe**: Haiman Faucet Style (`https://www.haimanfaucet.com`) - High-contrast editorial layout, excessive white space, refined typography, and macro focus on solid brass & stainless steel craftsmanship.
- **Color Palette**:
  - `--bg-primary`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
  - `--bg-secondary`: `#F8FAFC` (Cool slate gray for subtle contrast)
  - `--primary`: `#000000` (Pure industrial black for ultra-premium typography)
  - `--primary-accent`: `#64748B` (Neutral slate steel for labels)
  - `--border-subtle`: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` (Extremely thin lines as boundaries)

## 2. High Interactivity Specs
- **Category Filter**: Instantaneous, fluid CSS opacity and transform transitions.
- **Precision Image Framing**: Transparent or white backdrops only. Slight 3D depth change on hover (+1%).
- **Interactive Lead Drawer**: Flat-cut minimal overlay modal with stark borders and instant submission validation.
