# DESIGN.md - Stark Bathroom Luxury Industrial Dark Design Contract

## 1. Product Goal & Brand Identity
- **Goal**: Reconstruct Nan'an Yingdu Stark Hardware into a premier global B2B luxury industrial sanitary brand.
- **Vibe**: Indestructible, high-end, contract-spirit manufacturing.
- **Reference Style**: Luxury Industrial Dark (inspired by premium custom armor and micro-precision heavy-duty hardware).

## 2. Visual Token System
- `--bg-body`: `#090D16` (Deep volcanic basalt)
- `--bg-header`: `rgba(10, 14, 26, 0.85)` (Glassmorphic dark steel blur)
- `--bg-card`: `#131929` (Titanium slate for product cards)
- `--primary`: `#D4AF37` (Brushed Champagne Gold)
- `--primary-hover`: `#E9C46A` (Bright Sunlit Gold)
- `--text-light`: `#F8FAFC` (Pure Titanium Off-white)
- `--text-muted`: `#94A3B8` (Slate Grey)
- `--border-gold`: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)` (Gold thread boundary)

## 3. Interaction & Motion Specs
- **Hover Micro-movements**: Cards scale up by `1.03` with a glowing golden box-shadow shadow.
- **Language Switch Drawer**: Floating modular drawer with deep glass-blur and gold trim.
- **Inquiry Modal**: Translucent dark overlay with gold borders and active green confirmation.
